Questions

FAQ

You do not build pipelines or automations: you chat with Devoku through a simple chat layer and it acts instantly, rather than only answering.

You describe the job in chat or by voice, and it sets up its own environment and its own automation instead of borrowing a chat window for an hour.

Nearly anything you can ask it to do or automate, as long as the work is digital.

For ideas, see the job examples page: 42 examples are listed there, and it is not limited to them. It is built universally, to do what you need.

When it needs details from you, such as logins and passwords for closed sources it has to reach to finish the job, it asks you to paste them securely into the encrypted Devoku Secrets Vault in that agent’s Brain, which is built on OpenBao, instead of pasting them into the chat.

They are encrypted with OpenBao, resolved only when a run needs them, and masked in the interface.

Yes, by default. On an individual account, a collaborator sees only the Devoku employee you invite them to, and only at the level you give them: read-only, or read and write.

A team account can be configured to view other people’s workspaces in your organization without adding them as collaborators.
