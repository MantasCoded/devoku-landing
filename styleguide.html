Accessibility rules

  • Body copy uses --c-body or darker (≥ 4.5:1 on white).
  • Orange and green are never used for small text; eyebrows use the -ink pairs.
  • Focus ring is always visible on keyboard navigation (2px, offset 3px).
  • Decorative collages are aria-hidden; product screenshots carry empty alt because they are illustrative.
